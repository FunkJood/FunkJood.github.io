Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Controller

The controller is the developer listed as "Seller" on the Knobelblock App Store page, where full seller details are shown. Contact: via the contact page (free GitHub account required).

Summary

Knobelblock needs no account and stores your progress only on your iPhone. We do not collect personal data ourselves. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

Game data

Scores, levels, coins and settings are stored locally on your device only.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads via Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited). Google may process your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only with your permission), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions, and crash and performance data. We ask for consent via Google's consent dialog (EEA/UK) and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Without consent only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice in the game settings ("Datenschutz-Einstellungen") and in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See Google's policy. Purchasing "Knobelblock Premium" stops all ads.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment data.

Duel mode

Two nearby devices connect directly via Bluetooth/Wi-Fi without internet. Only the chosen player name and game moves are exchanged between the two devices.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
